Please ensure you attempt all ACT questions.

(2.) James and John are brothers that live together in the same room.
They intend to paint a wall of their room.
Working alone: James can paint the wall in ten minutes while John can paint it in eight minutes.
Working together, determine the time it will take both of them to paint the wall.


Recall:
how long = time

$rate = \dfrac{amount\;\;of\;\;work}{time\;\;to\;\;do\;\;the\;\;work}$

work = paint a wall = 1 task
time for James = 10 minutes
time for John = 8 minutes
Let the time for both of them to complete the task = x
total rate = sum of individual rates

$ \underline{Working\;\;alone} \\[3ex] James:\;\; rate = \dfrac{1}{10} \\[5ex] John:\;\; rate = \dfrac{1}{8} \\[5ex] \underline{Working\;\;together} \\[3ex] total\;\;rate = \dfrac{1}{x} \\[5ex] \implies \\[3ex] \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{1}{10} + \dfrac{1}{8} \\[5ex] LCD = 40 \\[3ex] \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{4}{40} + \dfrac{5}{40} \\[5ex] \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{4 + 5}{40} \\[5ex] \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{9}{40} \\[5ex] \dfrac{x}{1} = \dfrac{40}{9} \\[5ex] x = \dfrac{40}{9} \\[5ex] x = 4.444444444\;minutes \\[3ex] OR \\[3ex] 60\;seconds = 1\;minute \\[3ex] 0.444444444 * 60 = 26.66666667 \approx 27 \\[3ex] x = 4\;minutes\;\;and\;\;27\;seconds \\[3ex] $ It will take James and John about 4 minutes and 27 seconds to paint the wall of their room.
